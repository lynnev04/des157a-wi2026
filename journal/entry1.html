Entry #1

January 18, 2026

After reading the article “Best practices for form design” by Salim Ansari, there were different methods designers could use to improve usability within forms that I had not considered before. One of these methods was to ask easy questions first and ask more complex questions at the end. Users are more likely to complete the form when they are given simple questions first. Based on personal experience, it feels easier to complete a form when you have gotten the easier stuff out of the way. Another method that I had not considered before was to not rely on color as the only visual way to communicate status. Although it is useful, it can be difficult for color blind people to navigate. As an example, an error message would have a red text color with an exclamation icon.

A website that I believe demonstrates best practices is the Bank of America website. When creating a new account, it uses both color and symbols to show users each of their specific password requirements. A checkmark appears next to the password requirement if the user has completed it.
